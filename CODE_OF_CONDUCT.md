# Code of conduct

BiBrain follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Explain your reasons and stay open to being wrong.
- Give feedback that is specific and useful, and take it in good faith.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any other behaviour that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space, including issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews. It also applies when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer, [@Rishflips](https://github.com/Rishflips), on GitHub. Reports are handled privately.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions that break these standards. Depending on how serious the behaviour is, they may give a warning, ask someone to step back for a while, or ban them from the project.
