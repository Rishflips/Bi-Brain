# BiBrain pricing proposal — 21 October 2026

Status: recommendation for owner review. No price, allowance, live-test budget,
provider product, checkout or paid processing has been activated by this document.
Amounts below are USD; consumer taxes and regional checkout amounts may differ.

These prices do not prove willingness to pay for BiBrain. In particular, Recall's
free agent access means our sales story must emphasize the whole managed suite:
continuity, inspectable and correctable memory, explicit project boundaries,
source-backed preparation, and reliable always-on operation once deployed.

## Recommended launch matrix

| BiBrain offering    | Monthly      | Annual total  | Launch scope                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------- | ------------ | ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Self-hosted edition | $0 software  | $0 software   | Useful core, local data, reviewed memory, scoped agent access and export. The user supplies the machine and any model costs. Public distribution still needs the license decision.                  |
| BiBrain Personal    | **$12**      | **$120**      | One managed personal brain, supported agents, source-linked reviewed memory, recovery and bounded AI assistance after the live/hosted gates pass. Same included processing budget on both cadences. |
| BiBrain Pro — later | $24 proposed | $240 proposed | Only introduce after measuring heavier demand and delivering larger processing/storage/automation limits. This tier is not implemented or approved.                                                 |

Start with one paid plan and two renewal choices. The current authority already
supports that structure. Adding multiple paid tiers needs an explicit catalog /
entitlement design; do not present Pro as a working checkout option now.

Personal's annual price saves $24 (16.7%) against twelve monthly payments.
Avoid lifetime deals and permanent founder discounts while AI and hosting costs
remain unmeasured. Do not promise unlimited paid reasoning or a fixed number of
intelligent actions before a representative live workload has been measured.

## Proposed internal safeguards

- Included model-provider cost ceiling: **$2 per subscriber per month**, on both
  monthly and annual subscriptions; monthly reset, no rollover or auto-overage.
- Suggested per-job ceiling: **$0.10**; suggested user-day ceiling: **$0.30**.
  These are proposed guardrails, not evidence that the selected models fit them.
- Suggested initial live evaluation ceiling: **$10 total**, only after explicit
  owner approval, verified current provider pricing and secure credential setup.
- Preserve captures, manual review, correction, export and agent access when
  included paid processing is exhausted. Explain the pause plainly.

Keep infrastructure dollars and tokens out of the main sales experience. Once
real workload costs are known, explain the included allowance using meaningful
actions and publish a clear limit; do not silently convert a $2 guardrail into an
unsubstantiated “unlimited AI” promise. Storage quotas and any hosted free trial
must also be specified and implemented before they appear in sales copy.

## Illustrative unit economics, not measured margins

[Dodo's pricing page](https://dodopayments.com/pricing) advertises a base fee of
4% + $0.40 per transaction. The examples assume only that base fee, a fully used
$2 monthly AI allowance, and a **hypothetical $1/month** hosting/operations cost.
They exclude salaries, support labor, customer acquisition, refunds, chargebacks,
currency/international/payment-method surcharges and tax effects. Actual merchant
terms and measured production costs must replace these assumptions.

| Scenario                  |            Revenue per month |     Base fee allocation | AI ceiling | Hypothetical hosting | Remainder before excluded costs |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------: | ----------------------: | ---------: | -------------------: | ------------------------------: |
| Personal paid monthly     |                       $12.00 |                   $0.88 |      $2.00 |                $1.00 |                   $8.12 (67.7%) |
| Personal paid $120 yearly | $10.00 recognized equivalent | $5.20/year ÷ 12 = $0.43 |      $2.00 |                $1.00 |                   $6.57 (65.7%) |
| $9 discount sensitivity   |                        $9.00 |                   $0.76 |      $2.00 |                $1.00 |                   $5.24 (58.2%) |

Recommendation: validate **$12/month or $120/year** with real pilot users. Keep
$24 Pro as a later expansion, and retain the free self-hosted route. Price reflects
the intended managed service; it is not a statement that hosted delivery, live AI
quality, legal terms or real Dodo payments are already release-ready.
