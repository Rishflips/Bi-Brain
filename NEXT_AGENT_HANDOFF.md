## 6. Recent defects that must not be reintroduced

### Readiness heartbeat race — repaired

The server sampled `now` **before** expensive policy/profile reads. An independent
worker could refresh its heartbeat during that interval, and the old timestamp
misclassified the fresh lease as future-dated. Parent reproduced the resulting
availability/consent flip during real synthetic long-call process tests.

Both readiness readers now accept a trusted clock function sampled **after reading
the lease**. Production server, worker, and direct decision-stage coordinator use
this seam. Fixed `Date` callers remain deterministic and strict. No clock-skew
grace, TTL extension or future-heartbeat exception was added. Job lease and money
checks retain their own actual timestamps. Do not replace this with longer TTLs,
weaker assertions or eager clock sampling.

Relevant files: `packages/runtime/{worker-readiness,decision-worker-readiness,
managed,managed-activation}.ts`, `apps/server/main.ts`, `apps/worker/main.ts`,
`apps/worker/managed-accounting.ts`. Regression coverage is in worker-readiness,
managed-consent-review, managed-decision-stage and actual-process suites.
